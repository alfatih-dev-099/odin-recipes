Egyptian salad

Description

Egyptian salad AKA salata baladi is one of the most famous kebab restaurants salads in Egypt. A delicious mix of crunchy vegetables, fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes
  • cucumber
  • carrots
  • red onion
  • Jalapeno
  • cayenne peppers

Steps

  1. Using a mortar and pestle crush garlic with cayenne pepper, cumin, coriander, salt, black pepper and half the jalapeño if using.
  2. On another plate, grate two tomatoes.
  3. In a large bowl combine garlic mixture and grated tomatoes.
  4. Pour lemon juice and vinegar and mix well.
  5. Add diced cucumber, tomatoes, onion, shredded carrot and the remaining jalapeño.
  6. Pour in water and add crushed ice, mix everything well, cover and refrigerate for 2 hours.
